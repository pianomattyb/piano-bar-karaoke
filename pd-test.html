Package test checklist

Click through everything below and note anything that looks wrong, broken, misspelled, or confusing.

  1. Read the proposal. Does it read right? Any typos, wrong details, or anything confusing?
  2. On the request page, search for a song and request it with your name. It should appear in the Requests list with your name, and grey out in the song list with “Requested by” and your name under it.
  3. Try tapping that same song again. It should be blocked — greyed out, no form opens.
  4. Watch the counter at the top of the song list. It reads “N of 20 requests this hour” — the hour holds 20 requests max, first come first served. At 20 it says “Requests are full” and the submit button locks.
  5. Try it on your phone as well as your computer. Anything that looks off on one and not the other is worth noting.
