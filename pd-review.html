What it is

Professional development days are full of seminars and speakers. This is the hour everyone actually remembers. Piano Bar Karaoke brings a live piano into your staff room, gym, or hall for one hour: your teachers pick the songs ahead of time from the song list — and the teacher who picks a song is the one who sings it, with Matthew playing live on piano while the room sings along — solo, in duets, or all together. No auditions, no experience needed, nobody put on the spot. Just a room full of teachers having fun together.

How it works

Three steps, zero prep for you

  1. STEP 1Pick your date

    A professional development day, a lunch hour, or a break between sessions.

  2. STEP 2Teachers choose the songs they’ll sing

    Browse the song list and claim the songs they’ll sing, ahead of time, at pianomattyb.github.io/piano-bar-karaoke.

  3. STEP 3Show up and sing

    Matthew brings the piano and runs the hour. All you need is a room.

Why it works for a PD day

An hour your teachers will talk about

  • Real team building. Singing together breaks the ice faster than any icebreaker.
  • Everyone can join in. Solo stars, duets, or the whole room in chorus. Nobody has to sing alone.
  • Zero prep for organizers. The song list, the piano, and the hour are all handled.
  • A genuine break. An hour of fun between seminars — not another session.

What’s included

  • A song list — your teachers browse it and pick the songs they’ll sing.
  • Piano and sound provided.
